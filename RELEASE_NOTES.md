# v0.6.1

## Curium for Desktop (Public Pre-release)

Curium is now available for Windows, macOS, and Linux. Generate, customize with full control over every detail — colors, eye shapes, pixel patterns, and more. Everything runs locally on your machine.

### Highlights
- Deep QR customization: colors, eye shapes, pupils, pixels, gradients, and logos
- Batch generation: create multiple QR codes at once from a list
- Templates: save and reuse your favorite styles
- History: browse and restore previous QR codes
- SVG and PNG export: vector quality for print, raster for sharing
- Three themes: Dark, Light, and AMOLED
- Keyboard shortcuts for every action
- Works offline: no internet connection needed
- No admin rights required to install on Windows

### Known Limitations
- QR scanning is not yet available on desktop
- Some visual refinements are still in progress

## Mobile v0.6.0

### What's New
- Share to Curium: send QR codes from other apps directly into Curium
- Gallery scan: pick an image from your gallery and scan it
- Licenses screen: view all open-source libraries powering Curium
- WiFi password toggle: tap the eye icon to show or hide your password

### Improvements
- Camera performance improvements
- Smoother animations throughout the app
- Updated to Expo SDK 57

### Fixes
- Camera crash on launch fixed
- Share overlay dismisses properly
- Various animation and UI fixes
